The Biggest End-of-Year Kids Party at Rays of Grace Arena – Don't Let Your Child Miss Out!

  • 12 Nov 2026
  • System Admin
  • Kids Party

Parents, this one is for your kids! If you have a child, then Friday, 27th November 2026 is a day you cannot afford to skip. Rays of Grace Arena is set to host the BIGGEST END-OF-YEAR KIDS PARTY of the season — and it promises to be an unforgettable celebration for every child who walks through the gates. 🥳

A Day Made Just for the Little Ones
Every parent knows the joy of seeing their child light up with excitement, and this party is designed to deliver exactly that. From the moment the gates open, children will be treated to a day packed with fun, laughter, and memories that will last well into the new year. Whether your child loves games, music, dancing, or simply spending time with friends, this end-of-year celebration has something special in store for everyone.

The event will take place at the well-known Rays of Grace Arena, a venue that has hosted countless memorable moments for families in the community. With ample space and a festive atmosphere, it's the perfect setting for children to let loose and celebrate the end of another school year.

Event Details at a Glance
📅 Date: Friday, 27th November 2026
📍 Venue: Rays of Grace Arena
⏰ Gates Open: 11:00 AM
💰 Entrance: Only 20K
🍽️ Food (Optional): 10K

The entrance fee is just 20,000 shillings, making it an affordable way to give your child a day they'll never forget. For those who want to make the experience even more complete, an optional food package is available for just 10,000 shillings — so you can rest assured your little one will be well fed and happy throughout the day.

A Message to Every Mummy, Daddy, Auntie, and Uncle
Here's the truth every parent knows too well: no child wants to be the one who hears that everyone else went while they stayed home. The excitement of a party is only half the fun — the other half is being there to experience it with friends.

So to every mummy, daddy, auntie, and uncle out there: don't let your kid hear that others went and they missed it! Bring them ALL! Whether you have one child or a whole crew, this is the day to gather them up and head to Rays of Grace Arena for a celebration they'll be talking about for months.

Don't Wait – Mark Your Calendar!
The end of the year is a time for celebration, and there's no better way to wrap up 2026 than with a party dedicated entirely to your children. Mark Friday, 27th November 2026 on your calendar, gather the little ones, and make your way to Rays of Grace Arena from 11:00 AM.

This is more than just a party — it's a chance for your child to close out the year with joy, laughter, and memories that will bring a smile to their face for years to come. Spaces will fill up fast, so don't delay. Pay via MoMo code 006613, call 0706920587 or 0781449351 for any inquiries, and get ready to give your kids the end-of-year celebration they truly deserve!

See you at Rays of Grace Arena — bring them ALL! 🎉
