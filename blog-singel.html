Hon. Anita Annet Among as Chief Guest – 10 Years of Rays of Grace

  • 27 Sep 2025
  • System Admin
  • Education

Rays of Grace Junior School was greatly honored to welcome the Rt. Hon. Anita Annet Among, Speaker of the Parliament of Uganda, as the Chief Guest during its 10-year anniversary celebrations. Her presence added prestige and inspiration to this important milestone in the school's journey of nurturing young learners.

During the event, the Speaker commended the school's leadership, staff, parents, and learners for their commitment to excellence over the past decade. She encouraged the pupils to stay focused on their studies and highlighted the value of discipline, hard work, and integrity in shaping a bright future. The celebration also showcased the school's achievements, including improvements in academic performance, expansion of facilities, and the strengthening of co-curricular programs.

Her participation not only uplifted the ceremony but also reaffirmed the school's growing impact in the community. The 10-year anniversary marked a moment of reflection, gratitude, and renewed commitment to providing quality education for all learners at Rays of Grace Junior School.
