About us

Rays of Grace Junior School

Rays of Grace Junior School is a registered Mixed Day and Boarding Primary School in Njeru, Buikwe District, Uganda. Founded in 2015, the school is built on the motto "Knowledge Changing Lives Forever".

Beyond academics, the school integrates programs like Music, Computer and digital literacy, and sports especially through its wider Rays of Grace Academy. The school is also known for supporting vulnerable and disadvantaged children through bursaries.

Meet Our Team

The staff of Rays of Grace Junior School is a dedicated, professional and mission-driven team committed to providing a holistic learning experience for every child. They include qualified teachers, support staff and administrators who work together to maintain high academic standards.

Beyond classroom teaching, the staff plays an active role in shaping learners' character, discipline and social skills. We support extracurricular activities such as music, sports and computer literacy.
