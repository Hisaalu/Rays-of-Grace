Program Summary

This is a blended learning program, combining live online classes with optional on-campus practical sessions at Rays of Grace Junior School. Learners study Microsoft Word (typing and formatting), Excel (data, formulas and charts), PowerPoint (presentations), and Access (database basics), alongside essential software installation skills.

All live sessions are hosted on our e-learning platform (ROGELE), with recordings available for revision. On-campus labs are scheduled on selected Saturdays for hands-on practice, troubleshooting, and final assessment. Learners need their own laptop and a stable internet connection to fully participate.

Requirements

1. A personal laptop (Windows 10 or later recommended)

2. Stable internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended)

3. Microsoft Office installed (2016 or later) — installation support provided

4. Working webcam and microphone for live online sessions

5. Zoom / Google Meet capable device (or access to ROGELE live classroom)

6. An active email address for ROGELE account registration

7. Basic English reading ability

8. Commitment to attend online sessions and complete practice tasks

How Blended Learning Works

Online: Live lessons, recorded tutorials, assignments and quizzes delivered through ROGELE. Learners can learn from anywhere.

On-Campus: Selected lab sessions at Rays of Grace Junior School for practical work, installation practice, and the final examination.

Learn Microsoft Office alongside Software Installation — delivered in a blended format
  • Lesson 1.1
  • Computers, Computer Shortcuts & Microsoft Office
  • 02 days

In this lesson, one will be introduced to different computer parts, shortcuts, Microsoft Office programs, opening and closing office applications, saving, managing and opening files and folders. Delivered online via ROGELE.

  • Lesson 1.2
  • Microsoft Word & Mail Merging
  • 03 days

Here one will work with text editing, images, creating tables, page layout and simple templates. The last bit will be understanding the mail merging process and mail merging. Practical exercises submitted online.

  • Lesson 1.3
  • Microsoft Excel — Basics
  • 02 days

You will understand rows, columns and cells. Enter data in cells, format cells, use simple formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, PRODUCT, DIFFERENCE), V-Lookup and create multiple sheets.

  • Lesson 1.4
  • Excel Charts & Data Handling
  • 03 days

Create charts, sort & filter data and perform data analysis. Learn how data drives decision making. Data projects submitted via ROGELE.

  • Lesson 1.5
  • Microsoft PowerPoint
  • 03 days

Create a new presentation, add slides, choose layouts, insert texts, images and shapes, apply slide transitions and animations, prepare and present a slideshow — final presentation delivered live online.

  • Lesson 1.6
  • Introduction to Access & Other Databases
  • 03 days

Definition of a database, create tables in Access, fields and data types, entering and viewing records, simple queries and forms.

  • Lesson 1.7
  • Software Installation (On-Campus Lab)
  • 02 days

Practical session on installing Microsoft Office, drivers and common software. Delivered on-campus at Rays of Grace Junior School — bring your own laptop.

  • Assessment
  • Microsoft Office & Software Installation
  • On-Campus

Test & Final Examination — held on-campus at Rays of Grace Junior School.
